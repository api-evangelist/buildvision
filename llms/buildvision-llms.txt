# BuildVision

> BuildVision is an AI bid-management and equipment-procurement platform for the construction industry. It monitors email and plan rooms for incoming bids, extracts equipment specifications from construction documents, matches them against a company's line card, and drafts quotes with conflict detection — keeping manufacturer reps and OEMs on top of every bid. This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public BuildVision surface; BuildVision does not publish a public developer API or docs.

## Products

- Bid Triage Automation — scores and sorts incoming bids with go/no-go recommendations.
- Spec Memory — retains historical engineer data and specifications across projects.
- Construction Equipment Quoting — drafts quotes from extracted specifications with RFI generation on conflicts.
- Channel Intelligence (OEMs) — visibility into channel and rep activity.
- Selection Software Integration (OEMs) — integrates with equipment selection software.
